## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Platform Definition

**Cancure** is a caregiver-first cancer journey organizer that solves the systemic fragmentation of cancer care. Unlike AI diagnosis platforms or hospital infrastructure systems, Cancure is a **coordination layer** for patients, caregivers, doctors, and hospitals.

The platform does **not** diagnose cancer, predict survival, or replace doctors. Instead, it provides:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Medical Vault** | Secure, structured storage for all cancer reports, prescriptions, and imaging |
| **Medication Continuity Engine** | Active tracking of prescriptions, refill alerts, and treatment completion workflows |
| **Doctor-Ready Brief** | One-page structured summary for any doctor, generated on demand |
| **Hospital Connectivity** | Secure shareable links and QR codes that work with **any hospital** (no integration required) |
| **Emergency Auto-Send** | One-tap transmission of patient records to the nearest capable hospital |
| **"What's Missing?" Engine** | Intelligent gap analysis that identifies missing reports, tests, or follow-ups |

**Cancure is not a marketplace, not a hospital SaaS, not an AI diagnostic tool, and not a pharmacy platform. It is a caregiver's single source of truth for the cancer journey.**

## 2. The Problem: Fragmentation of Cancer Care

### 2.1 The Core Problem

Cancer care in India is not a system – it is a collection of silos. A typical patient will accumulate:

- **20–40 reports** (blood tests, imaging, biopsies, pathology, genetic tests)
- **5–8 specialists** (surgeon, medical oncologist, radiation oncologist, radiologist, etc.)
- **3–5 hospitals** (diagnosis, treatment, second opinion, follow-up)
- **10–20 prescriptions** with varying dosages and durations
- **50+ follow-up appointments** over 6–24 months

**No single entity has a complete view.** The patient's health record exists in fragments across paper files, PDFs, WhatsApp messages, and separate EMRs that do not talk to each other.

**The caregiver becomes the sole integrator** – a role they were never trained for and a burden they did not choose.

### 2.2 The Four Types of Fragmentation

#### A. Information Fragmentation
- Reports are paper or PDFs, rarely structured
- No consistent labeling or extraction of key data (tumor size, markers, dates)
- Patients repeatedly recount their history at every new consultation
- Doctors lose time reconstructing care from scraps

#### B. Temporal Fragmentation
- Cancer care spans months or years
- Handoffs between phases (diagnosis → surgery → chemo → radiation → surveillance) are where information loss is most severe
- No system manages these transitions – caregivers must remember everything

#### C. Financial Fragmentation
- Treatment costs vary wildly by hospital, by drug (branded vs. generic), by stage
- Patients often discover too late that the same drug costs 95% less as a generic
- Insurance coverage is opaque and fragmented

#### D. Coordination Fragmentation
- Caregivers must schedule appointments, manage medications, transport patients, and communicate across providers
- When caregivers are overwhelmed, errors occur: missed appointments, misplaced reports, incorrect dosing, delayed care

### 2.3 The Imatinib Example (Pure Price Inefficiency)

| Version | Monthly Cost (INR) | Annual Cost (INR) | 5-Year Cost |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Branded (Gleevec) | ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,20,000 | ₹12L – ₹14.4L | ₹60L+ |
| Generic (Janaushadhi) | ₹2,000 – ₹10,000 | ₹24,000 – ₹1.2L | ₹3L |

**Savings: 95%** – Same molecule, same clinical outcome, same survival benefit.

### 2.4 Why Existing Solutions Failed

| Solution Category | Why It Failed |
|------------------|----------------|
| **EMRs (Epic, Cerner, Practo)** | Hospital-centric, not patient-centric. No caregiver access. No cross-hospital continuity. |
| **PHRs (Apple Health, Google Health)** | Passive storage only. No active alerts, no refill tracking, no doctor briefs. Shut down. |
| **Oncology AI Platforms (Flatiron, Tempus)** | Built for pharma research, not patient care. No caregiver coordination. |
| **Adherence Apps (Medisafe)** | Solve forgetfulness, not system failure. No refill intelligence, no pharmacy stock checks. |
| **Telemedicine (Practo, Apollo 24x7)** | Each consultation creates a new silo. No integrated history. |
| **Pharmacy Platforms (1mg, PillPack)** | Transactional, not longitudinal. No treatment completion workflows. |

**The common failure pattern:** They all tried to replace a piece of the system instead of **coordinating the pieces that already exist**.

## 3. The Cancure Solution

> **Cancure is a caregiver-first cancer journey organizer that turns scattered reports, prescriptions, consultations, and follow-ups into one structured timeline and one doctor-ready brief.**

The platform does **not**:
- Diagnose cancer
- Predict survival
- Recommend treatments
- Replace doctors
- Require hospital integration
- Sell drugs
- Process insurance claims

The platform **does**:
- Store and structure all medical records
- Track medications and trigger refill alerts
- Generate a one-page doctor-ready summary
- Connect to any hospital via shareable link or QR code
- Auto-send records to the nearest hospital in emergencies
- Identify missing reports and follow-ups

### 3.1 User Roles

| Role | Interaction with Cancure |
|------|--------------------------|
| **Caregiver** | Primary user. Uploads reports, tracks medications, shares with doctors and hospitals, receives alerts. |
| **Patient** | Passive recipient. Can confirm medication adherence via app or SMS. |
| **Doctor** | Receives doctor-ready brief via shareable link. No login required. No dashboard to manage. |
| **Hospital** | Receives patient records via QR code or link. No integration required. No login. |

## 4. Key Features

### 4.1 Medical Vault & OCR
- Upload reports (PDF, JPG, PNG, WhatsApp forward)
- OCR extracts: medicine names, dosages, dates, test values, doctor names
- All data stored with source image snippets for verification
- Confidence flags: "95% certain – verified" or "65% certain – please verify"

### 4.2 Medication Continuity Engine
- Automatically creates structured prescription schedule from uploaded images or manual entry
- Tracks remaining days in real time
- Escalating alerts at: **7 days, 5 days, 3 days, 1 day remaining**
- Notifications via push, SMS, in-app, WhatsApp (caregiver choice)
- **Treatment Review Workflow** – When course completes, asks: "Schedule follow-up consultation?"

### 4.3 Doctor-Ready Brief
- One-page structured summary (PDF + JSON)
- Sections: patient overview, timeline, active medications, alerts, missing reports
- Generated on demand, never stored permanently
- Shareable via WhatsApp, email, SMS, QR code

### 4.4 Hospital Connectivity (No Integration Required)
- Caregiver taps "Connect to Hospital"
- Generates secure, time-limited link (expires in 24 hours or after 5 views)
- QR code generated automatically
- Hospital staff scans QR or clicks link → **no login required** → sees read-only patient brief
- Works with **any hospital** – partner or not

### 4.5 Emergency Auto-Send
- One-tap "Emergency" button
- Gets GPS location
- Queries curated directory of oncology-capable hospitals within 50 km
- Auto-sends patient brief to nearest hospital's emergency WhatsApp/email
- Also sends to caregiver and emergency contact via SMS
- Displays ambulance numbers + Google Maps directions

### 4.6 "What's Missing?" Engine
- Intelligent gap analysis of the patient's timeline
- Flags: missing blood tests, overdue follow-ups, incomplete treatment courses, unreported surgeries
- Displayed as checklist in caregiver app and appended to doctor brief

## 7. Business Model & Revenue

### 7.1 Revenue Streams (Single, Aligned)

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | ₹0 | Vault (limited storage), timeline, basic doctor brief, shareable links |
| **Premium** | ₹300–500/month | Human verification of high-risk fields, unlimited storage, priority OCR, family sharing (3+ caregivers), medication alerts via SMS |
| **One-time** | ₹100 | Verified nurse review of a single report |

### 7.2 Why This Works

- **Caregivers already spend ₹50,000–2 lakh/month** on treatment. ₹300–500 is negligible.
- **Human verification is tangible** – caregivers pay for certainty, not for "AI features."
- **No sales cycle** – caregivers sign up directly. No hospital procurement, no doctor persuasion.
- **Retention** – Once a caregiver uploads 6 months of reports, they will not switch.

### 7.3 What We DO NOT Monetize

| Monetization Model | Why Rejected |
|-------------------|---------------|
| Hospital SaaS | Long sales cycles, requires integration |
| Pharma marketplace | Ethical complexity, chicken-and-egg problem |
| Insurance API | Regulatory hurdles, misaligned incentives |
| Doctor subscription | Doctors are passive recipients – they won't pay |
